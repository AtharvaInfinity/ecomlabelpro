MEESHO ROUTE FIX

In your existing client/src/App.tsx (or the file where your React Router Routes are defined), add:

import MeeshoCropper from './pages/MeeshoCropper'

Inside <Routes> add:

<Route path="/meesho" element={<MeeshoCropper />} />

Your Navbar Meesho item should navigate to:

/meesho

Do not create a second BrowserRouter.
